01 / THE SHORT VERSION

AI that feels
nearby.

Orion is a small, approachable AI assistant. It can listen, reason, remember useful context, and take action through the tools you give it.

Think of it as a calm layer between you and the repetitive parts of digital life. You decide what it can see, what it can do, and when it should act.
